      Formulas

      • Bend allowance: BA = θ · (π/180) · (R + K·t)
      • Outside setback: OSSB = (R + t) · tan(θ/2)
      • Inside setback: ISSB = R · tan(θ/2)
      • Bend deduction: BD = 2 · OSSB − BA
      • Flat length: L = A + B − BD for outside dimensions, or L = A + B − 2·ISSB + BA for inside dimensions.

      Here θ is the angle the metal is turned through, not the included angle between the flanges. For several bends, add the straight legs and each bend’s BA.
